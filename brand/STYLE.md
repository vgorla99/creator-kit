# Kiln Hour — style guide

> Example brand. Kiln Hour is invented to show how Creator Kit works. Run `/brand-intake` in Claude Code to replace this file and `brand.yaml` with your own.

`brand.yaml` holds the facts (colours, handle, days). This file holds the feel: what a Kiln Hour reel looks, sounds and moves like. Claude reads both before planning a reel.

## In one line

Calm, close-up, honest pottery for beginners at a kitchen table. One tip per reel, the messy middle on show.

## Look

| Role | Colour | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| dark | `#1B1714` warm charcoal | deep background, text on light |
| light | `#F6EFE6` clay cream | light background, text on dark |
| primary | `#E8743F` terracotta | the one word that matters, the button |
| secondary | `#5E8F7E` celadon glaze | wipes, progress bars, small marks |
| tertiary | `#3B2850` plum underglaze | stat screens and the end card |

- Fonts: Space Grotesk for headlines, Inter for body, JetBrains Mono for labels and step numbers.
- Real footage first: hands, clay, tools, close and slightly top-down. Graphics frame the footage; they never replace it.
- One accent word per screen in terracotta. Never two.

## Sound and voice

- Warm, calm, a little nerdy about clay. Talks like a friend at the next table, not a teacher at the front.
- Short sentences. Name the exact tool or step ("a damp towel overnight", not "keep it moist").
- Say when something failed and why. Failures are a pillar, not an embarrassment.
- Never: "you're doing it wrong" hooks, gear-shaming, hype words.
- Sample caption: *"Cracked at the handle again. Turns out the fix is slip, patience and a damp towel overnight. Save this for your next clay night."*

## Motion

- Keep: spring pop on the accent word, slow push-in on hands, wipe reveal between steps.
- Avoid: glitch, RGB split, a whoosh on every cut.
- Pace: about 2.5 words per second on captions. Let the clay sound breathe; music is optional.

## Content

- Pillars: beginner techniques (pinch, coil, slab) · fixing cracks and failures · glazing without a kiln at home.
- Series: *Clay night in 60 seconds* · *Why did this crack?*
- Hooks: mistake · before/after · one-tool challenge.
- Posting: Tuesday, Thursday, Sunday (Europe/Lisbon). Trial reels on.
- End card: "New clay night every Thursday" + "Follow for more".
