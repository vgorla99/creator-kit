# Composition and story

## Frame

**rule of thirds**: place the subject on the lines dividing the frame into thirds. Dynamic, natural.
**centre framing**: subject dead centre. Bold, symmetric, works great in 9:16.
**symmetry**: mirrored layout. Calm, formal, satisfying.
**negative space**: deliberate empty area around the subject. Premium, focused, room for text.
**leading lines**: lines in the frame point to the subject.
**focal point**: the one place the eye goes first. Every frame has exactly one.
**depth layers**: foreground, midground, background. Three layers make even flat design feel deep.
**visual hierarchy**: size, contrast, colour and motion order what the eye reads first, second, third.
**scale contrast**: very big next to very small. Instant drama.
**aspect ratio**: 9:16 reels, stories and TikTok · 1:1 or 4:5 feed · 16:9 YouTube and web. Design for the main one; plan safe crops for the others.
**title/action safe**: margins where text and action stay safe from cropping and UI (see safe zone in type-captions).

## Pre-production

**style frame**: one finished still that defines the look before animating. Style Lab produces these.
**colour script**: the colour mood across the whole piece, scene by scene.
**storyboard**: sketches of each key shot in order. **animatic**: the storyboard timed to audio, rough motion. Approve timing here, not after rendering.

## Story beats

**hook**: the first 0.5–1.5s that stops the scroll: motion, contrast, a question or a bold claim. Never start on a logo for social.
**build**: tension or information grows. **reveal**: the key thing is shown (product, number, answer). **payoff**: the satisfying end moment.
**call to action (CTA)**: what to do next (follow, sign up, link). Hold it 1.5–3s with the handle or URL readable.

## Formats

**logo reveal**: animated logo appearance, 3–8s. **logo sting**: very short logo animation with a sound, 1–3s, for the end of videos.
**bumper**: short branded clip between segments. **explainer**: teaches one idea with simple visuals and voice-over.
**product hero**: the product as star: orbit, light sweep, slow push, detail close-ups.
**UI demo**: animated app interface: cursor, taps, zoom into features (motionmaxxing `references/ui-demo.md`).
**data viz animation**: charts and numbers that build in (bars grow, lines draw, number roll). Animate in the order the story is told.
